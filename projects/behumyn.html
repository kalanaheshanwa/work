BeHumyn — Wearable Recovery Technology Website

A fresh consumer-facing website for a first-of-its-kind recovery wearable.

Client
BeHumyn
Industry
Wearable Recovery Technology
Timeline
2 Weeks
Built on

Overview

Consumer wearables need a website that sells a feeling, not a spec sheet. BeHumyn needed to explain a new patent-pending recovery product in simple language and build excitement for launch.

Solution

I created a soft, modern Squarespace site with benefit-led headlines such as “Your relief, your way”, product education sections and a clear sign-up path. The layout keeps copy short and the product imagery large.
