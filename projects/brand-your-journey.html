Brand Your Journey — Brand Strategy Consulting Website

A persuasive, copy-driven Squarespace site for a brand strategy consultancy — built to turn skeptical visitors into booked calls.

Industry

Timeline

Overview

Brand Your Journey is a brand strategy consultancy serving nonprofits, startups, and small businesses under $2M — helping them define who they are, who they’re for, and why it matters. They needed a persuasive, conversion-focused website that clearly articulated the value of brand strategy to an audience that often underestimates it.

Solution

Designed and developed a sharp, copy-driven Squarespace website built to educate and convert. The site features a problem-aware homepage that speaks directly to the pain of inconsistent branding, a clear three-step process section, audience segmentation for nonprofits, startups, and small businesses, a blog (“The Strategy Room”), client testimonials, and a free consultation CTA — all structured to take skeptical visitors from “do I really need this?” to booking a call.
