By Lena — Photography Portfolio

A warm, image-first bilingual portfolio for a Munich photographer and videographer.

Client
By Lena
Industry
Photography, Videography
Duration
2 Weeks
Stack
Squarespace

Overview

By Lena is a Munich-based photographer and videographer specialising in weddings, family, lifestyle, travel and analog photography. She needed a warm, personal portfolio that felt as natural and authentic as her photography style — one that would attract clients across Germany and internationally.

Solution

Designed and developed a clean, image-first Squarespace portfolio that puts Lena’s photography front and centre. The site features separate gallery sections for each photography niche, a bilingual about section (German & English), client testimonials and a contact form — all wrapped in a warm, approachable aesthetic that reflects her personal brand.
