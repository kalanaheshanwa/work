Fletcher Films — Wedding Videography

A cinematic, editorial site for a UK luxury wedding videographer — built to let the films take centre stage.

Client
Fletcher Films
Industry
Photography, Videography
Duration
2 Weeks
Stack
Squarespace

Overview

Fletcher Films is a UK-based luxury wedding videography brand with nearly a decade of experience filming across the UK, Europe and beyond. They needed a refined, cinematic website that reflected the emotional storytelling at the heart of their work — not just showcasing weddings, but capturing the feeling of them.

Solution

Designed and developed an elegant Squarespace website with a minimal, editorial aesthetic that lets the films take centre stage. The site features a curated wedding-films gallery, a weddings showcase, an about page and a contact/availability form — all crafted to evoke emotion, build trust and convert high-end couples into inquiries.
