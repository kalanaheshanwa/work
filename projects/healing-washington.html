Healing Washington — Therapist Website on Squarespace

A calm therapy practice website that helps trauma and addiction clients feel safe reaching out.

Client
Healing Washington
Industry
Mental Health & Addiction Therapy
Timeline

Built on

Overview

People searching for online trauma or addiction therapy are often anxious and compare several therapists quickly. The practice needed a website that explained its specialities, built trust and made telehealth contact simple.

Solution

I created a soft, reassuring Squarespace site that opens with the key service, online trauma and addiction therapy for individuals, couples and families, then adds therapist background, specialities and a clear contact route. Search-focused titles and descriptions help local and telehealth searchers find the practice.
