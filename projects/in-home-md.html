Overview

In-Home MD is a Memphis-based independent medical practice delivering personalised primary care directly to seniors and older adults in their homes. Operated by a trusted physician moving to independent practice, they needed a professional, compassionate website to reassure existing patients and attract new ones — clearly communicating services, credibility and how to get started.

Solution

Built a full multi-page Squarespace website with a clean, trustworthy aesthetic tailored to a senior healthcare audience. The site features dedicated pages for current patients (continuity of care), new patients (onboarding flow), a comprehensive services breakdown, a physician about page, testimonials and a HIPAA-conscious contact section — all structured to build confidence and convert both returning and first-time patients.
