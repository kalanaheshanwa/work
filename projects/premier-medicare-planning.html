A trust-building, conversion-focused Squarespace site for a Dayton Medicare agency — rebuilt from client HTML across 13 pages.

Overview

Premier Medicare Planning is an independent Medicare insurance agency based in Dayton, Ohio, helping seniors navigate Medicare Advantage, Supplement, and Part D plans across 15+ carriers at no cost to the client. They needed a professional, trust-building website that would simplify a complex topic, establish local credibility, and drive free consultation bookings.

Solution

Designed and developed a multi-page Squarespace website built from client-provided HTML files — converting and integrating custom-coded page content into a fully functional Squarespace site. The site features a conversion-focused homepage, dedicated service pages for Medicare Advantage, Supplement, Part D, and Turning 65 planning, a step-by-step enrollment guide, client testimonials, a blog, and a free consultation CTA — all structured to educate hesitant seniors and convert them into booked consultations.
