Steeple Glenn — Luxury 55+ Condominium Website

A high-converting single-page marketing site for a luxury 55+ condo development — built around urgency, exclusivity and tour bookings.

Client
Steeple Glenn
Industry
Real Estate
Timeline
1 Week
Built on

Overview

Steeple Glenn is a boutique luxury condominium development in Newark, Delaware, offering move-in ready 2 and 3-bedroom residences designed for 55+ active adults seeking a refined, maintenance-free lifestyle. With only 9 homes remaining and a limited-time $70,000 upgrade offer, they needed a high-converting marketing website that would create urgency and drive qualified tour bookings fast.

Solution

Designed and developed a full single-page Squarespace real estate marketing website built around urgency, exclusivity, and lifestyle aspiration. The site features a bold hero with limited availability messaging, premium upgrade highlights, three residence model showcases, amenities and interior finishes sections, an interior photography gallery, a home valuation tool, FAQ accordion, location highlights, and a multi-CTA contact flow — all engineered to move serious buyers from interest to inquiry.
