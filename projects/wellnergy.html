Wellnergy London Festival — Event Website with Ticket Sales

A high-energy festival website that turns visitors into ticket buyers for a three-day wellness event.

Client
Wellnergy
Industry
Wellness Festival
Timeline
3 Weeks
Built on
Squarespace

Overview

Festival websites lose ticket sales when dates, zones and sponsors are hard to find. Wellnergy needed one site that sold the experience, showed the five wellness zones and sent visitors straight to buy tickets.

Solution

I built a bold Squarespace event site with a scrolling “tickets now on sale” banner, dates above the fold, a clear breakdown of the five zones and a dedicated area for sponsors and exhibitors. Repeated ticket buttons and a mobile-first layout keep the buying path short.
