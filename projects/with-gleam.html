With Gleam — Booking-Focused Squarespace Rebuild

A complete Squarespace rebuild focused on stronger trust, clearer treatment choices and a smoother path to booking.

Client
With Gleam
Industry
Teeth Whitening Clinic
Timeline
3 Weeks
Built on
Squarespace

Overview

With Gleam's original website was functional but visually basic, with a weak information hierarchy and a booking journey that made it harder for visitors to understand treatments and take the next step. The clinic needed a more professional presence that reflected registered clinical care while improving local visibility and conversion.

Solution

I rebuilt the site around trust and booking intent: clearer treatment options, stronger clinical positioning, improved page structure, repeated booking actions and a cleaner Acuity scheduling journey. I also completed core SEO setup and prepared the site for local search and Google marketing, giving the client a more credible website that is easier to navigate and easier to book from.
