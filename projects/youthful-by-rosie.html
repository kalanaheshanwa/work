Youthful by Rosie — Figma to Squarespace Website

A complete Figma design translated into a responsive, editable Squarespace website without losing the original visual direction.

Client
Youthful by Rosie
Industry
Aesthetics & Wellness
Timeline
2 Weeks
Built on
Squarespace

Overview

Youthful by Rosie came with a complete Figma design and needed every page accurately recreated in Squarespace. The main challenge was preserving the refined typography, soft colour palette, imagery, spacing and treatment-driven layouts while ensuring the site remained practical to edit and worked smoothly across screen sizes.

Solution

I built the full website in Squarespace to closely match the supplied Figma files, recreating the page layouts, treatment sections, calls to action and content hierarchy in Fluid Engine. I then refined desktop, tablet and mobile behaviour so the finished site stays visually consistent with the design while remaining fully editable for the client.
